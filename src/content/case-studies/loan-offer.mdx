<CaseStudySection label="Project Overview">

After a company-wide rebrand, it was time to advance our offers experience and give the user more clarity, control, and understanding over what loan they should choose.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection label="Problem">

The offers page is one of the most important moments in the customer flow for Best Egg. The user is excited that they've been approved and can choose a loan that can help them along on their financial journey. However, the current experience creates heavy friction for the user.

<CaseStudyImage src="/images/case-studies/offers_page.png" alt="" />
<CaseStudyImage src="/images/case-studies/offer_details.svg" alt="" />
<CaseStudyPillList
  variant="problem"
  items={[
    "Loan details were difficult to compare",
    "Only two loans could be viewed together on mobile (60% of our users apply on mobile)",
    "Trust signals were weak at a high-stakes financial moment",
    "It was difficult to scale design to accompany multiple product lines",
  ]}
/>

These issues resulted in 40% drop off rate between offers page and verification next steps, and increasing customer support questions for our agents.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection label="Takeaways">

- Financial products require clarity above all, designing for financial decisions means prioritizing confidence and comprehension.
- Small hierarchy changes drive big impact, elevating the monthly payment dramatically improved decision speed.
